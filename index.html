Good to know

Questions before you start?

TutorsAtWork is an account-based learning app. Access is managed by your tutor or administrator.

Can I create my own account?

Student access is created and managed by a TutorsAtWork administrator. Ask your tutor for your sign-in details.

Which version should I download?

Choose the universal Android APK for Android 7.0 or newer. Choose the Windows x64 installer for supported 64-bit Windows 10 and Windows 11 computers.

What does the Windows installer include?

The installer sets up TutorsAtWork and checks the Microsoft Visual C++ and WebView2 runtime requirements used by the app.

Is Exam AI available to students?

No. Exam AI is currently a tutor-only feature inside the installed TutorsAtWork app.
